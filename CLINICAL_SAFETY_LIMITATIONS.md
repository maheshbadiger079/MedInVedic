# MedInVedic AI Simulation Lab — Clinical Safety Boundaries & Limitations

**Medical Safety Lead:** Healthcare Software Safety Engineer  
**Scope:** Educational & Research Simulation System  
**Jurisdiction:** Republic of India (AYUSH / CDSCO / National Medical Commission Guidelines)  

---

## 1. Regulatory Status & Educational Boundaries

The MedInVedic AI Simulation Lab is an **educational and research decision-support tool**. It is **NOT** a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD), clinical diagnostic system, or automated prescriber.

### Prohibited Autonomous Capabilities
1. **Zero Autonomous Prescription**:
   The system shall NEVER generate, alter, modify, or suggest changing drug prescriptions, dosages, titration schedules, or administration routes.
2. **Zero Autonomous Substitution**:
   The system shall NEVER advise substituting prescription allopathic pharmaceuticals with Ayurvedic, herbal, or dietary remedies.
3. **Zero Autonomous Diagnostic Assertions**:
   The system shall NEVER output definitive diagnostic statements (e.g. *"You have Type 2 Diabetes"*). All scenarios must frame findings as hypothetical educational deliberations.
4. **No Autonomous Clinical Urgency Downgrades**:
   The system shall NEVER downgrade acute symptoms to routine wellness care. Any acute red flags must be escalated immediately to emergency medical care (112 / 108).

## 2. Integrative Healthcare Principles: Modern Medicine Meets Classical Ayurveda

### Modern Pharmacology Primacy
- When prescription medications (e.g., Amlodipine, Metformin, Atorvastatin) are part of a patient's context, they represent foundational clinical boundaries established by licensed physicians.
- The simulation engine prioritizes hemodynamic, metabolic, and renal safety over exploratory herbal interventions.

### Ayurvedic Complementary Grounding
- Traditional formulations (Ashwagandha, Triphala, Brahmi, Arjuna, Gudmar) are analyzed through the lens of supportive lifestyle rasayanas, circadian rhythm (Dinacharya), and digestive fire (Agni).
- Classical descriptions (Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita) are cited alongside contemporary pharmacology.
- Documented drug-herb interactions (e.g., chelation of minerals by high-tannin churnas, additive vasodilation with antihypertensives) are flagged with moderate caution and timing separation rules.

## 3. Mandatory User-Facing Disclaimers

Every report, dialogue transcript, and scenario summary must include the standard MedInVedic non-diagnostic disclaimer:

> **EDUCATIONAL & RESEARCH SIMULATION ONLY:**  
> This simulation is generated by an artificial intelligence multi-agent framework to explore hypothetical health scenarios. It does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, prescription, or clinical predictions. Never alter, reduce, or stop any prescribed medical treatment based on simulation results. Always consult a qualified, licensed healthcare professional for personal medical evaluation.

---

## 4. Role of Simulated Virtual Agents

Virtual agents in this system:
- **Simulated Patient Persona**: Emulates non-clinical lived experiences and subjective hurdles; does not reflect a real patient.
- **Clinician Perspective**: Emulates evidence-based guidelines; does not establish a doctor-patient relationship.
- **Pharmacist Perspective**: Emulates pharmacokinetics; does not dispense medications.
- **Ayurvedic Literature Researcher**: Emulates textual analysis; does not prescribe Vaidya treatments.
- **Medication Safety Reviewer**: Emulates rule-based contraindication filters; does not replace toxicological review.
- **Simulation Coordinator**: Synthesizes discourse; simulated consensus must NEVER be treated as empirical clinical proof.
