# Privacy Policy for MedInVedic

**Last Updated: April 18, 2026**

MedInVedic ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose your information when you use our mobile application and website.

## 1. Information We Collect
*   **Personal Information:** When you sign in via Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture.
*   **Health Data:** Any health-related queries or symptoms you search for are used to provide recommendations and are stored securely.
*   **Usage Data:** We collect information about how you interact with our app (e.g., pages visited, clicks).

## 2. How We Use Your Information
*   To provide and maintain our service.
*   To personalize your experience (e.g., healthcare recommendations).
*   To communicate with you regarding updates or support.
*   To comply with medical data regulations (HIPAA/Vedic guidelines).

## 3. Data Sharing and Security
*   We **do not sell** your personal data to third parties.
*   Data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud).
*   Authentication is handled exclusively through Google Sign-In for maximum security.

## 4. Your Rights
*   You can request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting dev-support@medinvedic.com.
