Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

At MedInVedic, accessible from the MedInVedic app and website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by MedInVedic and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

If you use our services, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical delivery address.
  • Health Information: Prescriptions uploaded for ordering medicines and consultation history with doctors.
  • Payment Information: Transaction IDs and wallet balances (we do not store credit card numbers directly; they are handled by secure partners like Razorpay).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our platform.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our services.
  • Process your orders and manage consultations.
  • Communicate with you for customer service and updates.
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security (HIPAA/ISO standards).

3. Data Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your health data is encrypted and only accessible by authorized medical professionals during consultations.

4. Third-Party Services

MedInVedic may use third-party companies (e.g., Firebase for authentication, Razorpay for payments). These third parties have access to your personal information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the data we hold about you, or request that we delete any personal information we have collected.
