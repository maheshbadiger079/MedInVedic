🏥 Evidence-Based Dental Care (WHO & ADA Oral Health Guidelines)
• Modified Bass Brushing: Brush 2× daily for 2 minutes with a soft-bristled toothbrush angled at 45° toward the gingival sulcus.
• Interdental Cleaning: Daily interdental flossing prevents proximal contact decay and interpapillary gingivitis.
• Enamel Remineralization: Salivary flow stimulation through adequate hydration and chewing fibrous foods protects enamel calcium-phosphate balance.
• Prophylaxis Interval: Routine ultrasonic scaling recommended once every 6 months.
🌿 Ayurvedic Danta Swasthya (Charaka Samhita Sutrasthana Ch. 5)
• Kavala & Gandusha (Oil Pulling): Swish 1 tbsp lukewarm virgin sesame oil (Tila Taila) or coconut oil for 5–10 minutes on an empty stomach. Saponification neutralizes Streptococcus mutans and tightens periodontal ligaments (Asokan et al., JISP 2009).
• Danta Dhavana (Herbal Tooth Brushing): Utilize astringent/bitter herbal powders (Kashaya-Tikta Rasa) such as Neem (Azadirachta indica), Babool (Acacia arabica), and Clove (Lavanga) to arrest bacterial plaque biofilm.
• Triphala Mouthwash: Rinsing with cooled Triphala decoction provides proven anti-gingivitis and anti-plaque action comparable to 0.2% Chlorhexidine without tooth staining (Bajaj et al., 2011).
🌱 Salivary Microbiome & Dietary Guidelines
• Avoid Frequent Acid Exposure: Refined sugars and carbonated beverages foster acidogenic oral microflora (pH <5.5 leads to demineralization).
• Vitamin C & Bioflavonoids: Supports periodontal collagen cross-linking and vascular capillary resistance (Amla, citrus, bell peppers).
