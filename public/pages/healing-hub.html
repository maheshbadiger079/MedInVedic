RAG Daily Regimen

Your Daily Wellness Plan (Dinacharya)

Structured, evidence-grounded habits structured around your body's natural circadian rhythms.

🌅
Morning (Pratah)
  • Warm water hydration with lemon or ginger
  • 15-min gentle movement & Surya Namaskar
  • 5-min Anulom Vilom Pranayama
  • Warm nourishing breakfast (e.g. spiced oats / poha)
🌞
Afternoon (Madhyahna)
  • Primary meal of the day (highest Agni)
  • Balanced whole grains, dal, steamed greens
  • Short 5-min post-meal mindful walk
  • Sip warm cumin-coriander infusion
🌇
Evening (Sandhya)
  • Gentle joint mobility / 20-min walking
  • Light, early dinner before 7:30 PM
  • Avoid heavy fried foods or cold dairy
  • Mental wind-down from digital work
🌙
Night (Ratri)
  • Screen curfew 45 mins before bedtime
  • Optional Ashwagandha or warm spiced milk
  • Light meditation & gratitude reflection
  • Target 7.5–8 hours restorative sleep

Knowledge Library

Natural Remedy Explorer

Evidence-graded traditional formulations with clear preparation and safety guidelines.

Tulsi-Ginger Kadha Supported by Evidence

Traditional infusion for throat soothing, upper respiratory ease, and seasonal warmth.

Preparation: Boil 5 Tulsi leaves, 1g fresh crushed ginger in 200ml water for 5 minutes.
Sources: Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API) & WHO Guidelines
Triphala Churna Supported by Evidence

Classical 3-fruit formulation (Amalaki, Bibhitaki, Haritaki) for gentle bowel regularity.

Preparation: 1/2 teaspoon in warm water before bedtime.
Sources: Ministry of AYUSH AFI Monograph · CCRAS
Golden Turmeric Milk (Haldi Doodh) Traditional Practice

Warm bedtime restorative beverage combining curcumin with black pepper for enhanced absorption.

Preparation: 1/4 tsp organic turmeric + pinch of black pepper in warm milk.
Sources: Classical Ayurvedic Dinacharya & Nutrition Science
